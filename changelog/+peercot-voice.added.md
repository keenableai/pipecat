- Added a Voice PeerCoT example at `examples/peercot-voice/`. Two AI agents (Expert + Curious Thinker) hold a multi-turn spoken discussion on any topic, grounded by live Keenable web search and delivered through Cartesia TTS with per-speaker voice switching. Supports xAI Grok, Groq, and OpenAI as LLM providers. Inspired by the PeerCoT paper (Chaturvedi et al., ICLR 2026 Workshop).
